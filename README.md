## 🧠 What is CyberDrill?

CyberDrill is an interactive ransomware incident simulation designed to recreate the first critical hour of a cyberattack.

Instead of static training slides or multiple-choice quizzes, users experience:

- 💬 Real-time emergency chat communication
- ⚡ Adaptive incident evolution
- 🧩 Branching crisis scenarios
- ⏱ Time pressure and cascading failures
- 🛡 Incident response decision-making

Every action impacts the spread of the attack, business continuity, and the final outcome.

---

## 🎬 Scenario Overview

It’s Tuesday, 09:14 AM.

An employee from the purchasing department opened a malicious attachment disguised as a supplier invoice.

The payload silently deployed **LockBit 3.0**.

Now:
- files are encrypted,
- systems are failing,
- panic is spreading internally,
- and the ransomware is moving laterally across the network.

You are leading the incident response.

Good luck.

## 🎯 Why This Project Exists

Most cybersecurity awareness platforms fail because they are passive.

CyberDrill was created to make incident response:
- stressful,
- realistic,
- interactive,
- and memorable.

The goal is not only to teach procedures, but to simulate the psychological pressure of a real ransomware crisis.
